8. US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October and core prices 0.3%, above forecasts, lifting December rate-rise odds; no annual rate was published, as October 2025 data was never collected. (Reuters)
9. Anthropic set its IPO price range at $70 to $78 a share, to raise up to $64.35 billion at a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion, with Nasdaq trading expected around 19 November. (CNBC)
